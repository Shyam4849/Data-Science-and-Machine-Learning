Droupadi Murmu is the 15th and current President of India, having taken office on July 25, 2022.

Key details about the President of India:

Role: Nominal head of the executive, holding the highest constitutional office.

Term: Five years, residing at the Rashtrapati Bhavan in New Delhi.

Background: Born in 1958 in Odisha, she has a background in education and local politics.

Previous Holders: Notable predecessors include the first president, Dr. Rajendra Prasad, and the first female president, Pratibha Patil.
